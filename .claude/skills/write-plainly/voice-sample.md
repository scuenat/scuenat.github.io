# Voice sample: Stéphane's own handwriting

Transcribed from three handwritten pages (November 2026), the notes for "Chapter 1" and
"Chapter 2" of the *It must be simpler* idea. The grammar is kept close to the
original on purpose. **Copy the rhythm, the order of thought and the plain words. Do not
copy the grammar slips.** Stéphane writes English as a second language; the site corrects
grammar but keeps the voice.

**Ch 1.**

On that evening, I sat in my bed reading rapidly that paper, trying to get the idea even
if I am not a mathematician or a physicist, and even if I know it was like having
disrespect for that work. I could almost blame myself for having thought like that, but I
had that voice in my brain, from that ignorant mind, saying "it must be simpler".

Now, when I say "it must be simpler", I do understand that it cannot be as simple as that,
but like it someday we could be discovering that it is actually simpler.

Now, "simpler" in that sense does say that we may take a look, maybe simpler is as complex
as it is, but simpler as it is seen from a different perspective.

That change of perspective made me think of other aspects, not physics or maths, but it
may apply on what I know better: software engineering, and beyond that.

**Ch 2.** The experiment is about a simulation I used to test the idea (refer to my blog
for that and make it a bit more clear, and add more figures as needed).

Mainly it was about a fake dashboard for a team processing items, to show the "complexity"
of the ones we have nowadays, and how that apparent complexity is hiding a simple answer,
which is in that case, I believe, based on applying a different perspective on the board:
looking at the diffs (but check the blog to be more rigorous).

## What this sample shows

| Trait | In the sample |
|---|---|
| Starts from a moment, not a thesis | "On that evening, I sat in my bed reading…" |
| Says what he is not | "even if I am not a mathematician or a physicist" |
| Admits the doubt, then keeps going | "I could almost blame myself… but I had that voice" |
| The idea is a short sentence anyone could say | "it must be simpler" |
| Repeats the phrase, then sharpens it | "Now, when I say 'it must be simpler', I do understand…" |
| Defines a word in his own terms | "simpler… as it is seen from a different perspective" |
| Moves from the far field to what he knows | "not physics or maths… what I know better: software engineering" |
| Concrete, everyday objects | "a fake dashboard for a team processing items", "looking at the diffs" |
| Honest about confidence | "I believe", "check the blog to be more rigorous" |
| Signposts with "Now," | each new step starts "Now, …" |
